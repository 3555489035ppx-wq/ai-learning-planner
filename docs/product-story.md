# AI Learning Planner Product Story

## The problem

Students do not need an endless list of tasks. They need to make a defensible choice when several courses, goals, and interruptions compete for the same limited time.

## The product response

假期跃迁 treats planning as a system:

```text
Understand the starting point → Decide priorities → Place work → Execute → Capture evidence → Recover
```

The product keeps “what to learn” separate from “when to learn it”, so a plan can change its schedule without losing the learning intent.

## Why this is an AI product

The AI-shaped part of the product is not a chat window. It is the reasoning layer that turns incomplete evidence into a candidate diagnosis, prioritization, schedule, or recovery proposal. Every candidate carries its input, context, confidence, limitation, and confirmation step.

## Current boundary

The public demo uses deterministic local rules. This is intentional: the portfolio shows the product logic and human review contract without claiming that a remote model has already been evaluated.
